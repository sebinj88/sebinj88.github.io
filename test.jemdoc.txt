== About Me

I am an Assistant Professor in the [https://www.sdsmt.edu/academics/academic-departments/electrical-engineering-and-computer-science/index.html  Department of Electrical Engineering and Computer Science] at the [https://www.sdsmt.edu/index.html South Dakota School of Mines and Technology] in Rapid City, SD. 

Previously, I was a Junior Fellow in the Rice Academy of Fellows at  [https://www.rice.edu/ Rice University]
in Houston, TX. I was hosted by  [https://cauribe.rice.edu/ César Uribe]. Before going to Rice, I was a  postdoctoral scholar working with [https://people.kth.se/~hsan/ Henrik Sandberg] and [https://people.kth.se/~kallej/ Karl Henrik Johansson]
in the [https://www.kth.se/is/dcs/division-of-decision-and-control-systems-1.788078 Division of Decision and Control Systems] at the [https://www.kth.se/en KTH Royal Institute of Technology] in Stockholm, Sweden.

I received my Ph.D. in Automatic Control from [https://www.univ-grenoble-alpes.fr/ Université Grenoble Alpes] in 2018, under the joint supervision of [https://www.gipsa-lab.grenoble-inp.fr/user/alain.kibangou Dr. Alain Kibangou] and [https://necs.inrialpes.fr/people/garin/ Dr. Federica Garin]. Prior to that, I received an MS degree in Electrical Engineering from the [https://www.colorado.edu/  University of Colorado-Boulder] in 2013.

== Research Interests

- Stability analysis and control of  dynamic networked systems, with a particular emphasis on epidemiological networks.
- Theory of structured systems, with a focus on studying security in cyber-physical system


== Applications

- Public health
- Spread of rumors/opinions in social networks
- Resilient operation of infrastructure networks
