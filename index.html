About Me

I am an Assistant Professor in the Department of Electrical Engineering and Computer Science at the South Dakota School of Mines and Technology in Rapid City, SD.

Previously, I was a Junior Fellow in the Rice Academy of Fellows at Rice University in Houston, TX. At Rice, I was hosted by César Uribe. Before going to Rice, I was a postdoctoral scholar working with Henrik Sandberg and Karl Henrik Johansson in the Division of Decision and Control Systems at the KTH Royal Institute of Technology in Stockholm, Sweden.

I received my Ph.D. in Automatic Control from Université Grenoble Alpes in 2018, under the joint supervision of Alain Kibangou and Federica Garin. Prior to that, I received an MS degree in Electrical Engineering from the University of Colorado-Boulder in 2013.

Research Interests

  • Stability analysis and control of dynamic networked systems, with a particular emphasis on epidemiological networks.

  • Theory of structured systems, with a focus on studying security of cyber-physical systems

Applications

  • Public health

  • Spread of rumors/opinions in social networks

  • Product adoption in (online) marketplaces

  • Resilient operation of infrastructure networks
